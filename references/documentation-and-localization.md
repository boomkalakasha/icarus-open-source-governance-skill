# Documentation and localization

Public documentation must say what was actually tested, what remains `NOT_VERIFIED`, and how a maintainer can reproduce the claim. Pair English and Chinese navigation at the top of both READMEs, then keep installation, safety boundaries, support, security, and release statements behaviorally aligned.

Avoid placeholder personal contacts, claimed response times, invisible prerequisite services, and product claims that exceed local evidence. Prefer a concise support boundary over a fictional support promise. Templates are generic so repositories can select their own owner, locale, and community channels.
