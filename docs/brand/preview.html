Usage / 06

Keep the pulse legible.

Small choices protect the mark’s warmth and technical trust.

Do

Use the correct surface variant, preserve clear space, and keep the square avatar at or above 40 px.

Don't

Do not stretch, rotate, crop the nodes, add a mascot or explosive effect, or use the light wordmark on a pale surface.

Accessibility / 07

Contrast is part of the system.

Pair colour with meaning. The cyan and coral paths are expressive accents, not the only status signal. Keep copy in Midnight or Warm white, preserve the title and description in source SVGs, and add a visible project name when the symbol is used as navigation.
